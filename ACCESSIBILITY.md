# Accessibility

Neumorphic designs are known for low contrast. This theme tries to keep the soft look while staying readable and usable.

## What the theme does

- **Text contrast**: body text has a contrast ratio above 7:1 (WCAG AAA) in light and dark mode.
- **Accent and callout colors** reach at least 4.5:1 (WCAG AA).
- **Focus** stays visible for keyboard navigation.
- **Reduced motion**: transitions are turned off when your system asks for reduced motion, and can also be turned off in the Style Settings.

## Adjusting the theme

With the [Style Settings](https://github.com/mgmeyers/obsidian-style-settings) plugin you can make the relief stronger, change the base, text and accent colors, and turn off transitions.

## Feedback

If something is hard to read or use, for example with a screen reader, high zoom or a specific color setting, please [open an issue](https://github.com/LennZone/Neumorphism/issues/new/choose). Accessibility problems are treated as bugs.
